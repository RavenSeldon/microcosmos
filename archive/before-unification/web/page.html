The Dark Microcosmos

Six districts draw from one river. The Seventh Basin lives on what is left. You run a listening station. Survive six rounds and keep the basin alive. The best ending is to do it with no district going short.

How it works

Each round you take one action. Pick a district, then listen, signal or strike. Or hide. Keys: A to F pick a district; L, S, K, H act.

You see what districts do, not why. Each shows a low, medium or heavy draw from the river. Heavy can mean greed or real need. Listening tells you which.

Signal asks a district to live on its reserve for two rounds. It may agree, decline, or have too little stored. Strike stops a district acting on the network for one round, which can stop a threatened attack. Its water is not touched. The district may hit your station back once, that round, and will refuse your offers afterwards.

Exposure. Signalling adds 2 and striking adds 3; hiding removes 2. At 2 or more your station can be hit, and at 4 some districts will hit it unprovoked. It has 3 integrity.

Threats. A district may threaten a heavy extractor. The strike comes next round only if the target is still drawing heavily, and only if the attacker is able to act.

The basin needs 30 a round. Two short rounds in a row and it collapses. Lose the station and it is over too.

Endings. Survive with no district ever in distress: Shared Dawn. Survive otherwise: Fragile Peace. Fall: The Long Night. Then the world shows what was hidden.

Every world is fixed by its number: the same number and the same choices always play out the same way. Nothing here is random once a world begins, and nothing is sent anywhere. The rules are an experiment and will change.
