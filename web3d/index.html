The Seventh Basin speaks

The Dark Microcosmos

Six worlds share one basin. We are the seventh community, and you keep our water.

You cannot command a world. You can listen, ask two of them to share a current, tend one from our stores, ask one for what it does best, or wait.

Eighteen moments: an open sky, then the dark, then the rain’s return. Bring every world through awake, with room for the water it has used.

Nothing moves while you think. Listening is free. Every moment can be undone.

One journey, the same possibilities. Choose written guidance from the worlds, or discover for yourself. Change this in the keeper’s notes. Music is a separate, optional choice.
