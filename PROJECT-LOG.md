## Decisions of Record

- Simulator built on v0.3's numbers as provisional, not frozen (Ben, 14 Nov 2026).
- G1a (accounting only, no rule changes) built. Basis: Ben forwarded *G1 Decision Record · v0.4
  Experimental Plan* on 14 Nov 2026 with no comment, after being asked which decisions to accept.
  That was read as a go-ahead for G1a only. The record marks its other four decisions provisional;
  they remain open below.

- **Adopted by Ben, 14 Nov 2026 (recorded in Ben's wording):**
  1. Cooperation should generally create better shared outcomes through actual avoided harm, rather
     than arbitrary rewards.
  2. Victory remains survival through six resolved rounds. Shared Dawn, Fragile Peace, and Long
     Night are consequence-sensitive endings; their thresholds remain provisional.
  3. Ambient violence should be less frequent and more understandable. Replace immediate unprovoked
     preemption with publicly telegraphed, conditional threats.
- **G1b rule, specified by Ben, 14 Nov 2026:** (1) Trigger: a Preemptive district observing another
  district in the high-extraction band may issue a public threat, using the existing deterministic
  priority rules. (2) Warning: the threat identifies attacker, target, condition, and the next round
  as its resolution time. (3) Resolution: after the player's next action, calculate the target's
  planned withdrawal; if it remains high and the attacker can act, the strike proceeds, otherwise
  the threat lapses. (4) Intervention: SIGNAL may reduce the target's withdrawal; STRIKE may disable
  the attacker; neither guarantees success without satisfying the existing rules. (5) Accounting:
  record threatened, executed, cancelled, and interrupted attacks separately.
- **Authorised sequence (Ben, 14 Nov 2026):** G1b (conditional threat, network-slot isolation,
  focused leverage regression), then G1.5 (ugly 2D playable). Explicitly deferred: relay-cap
  tuning, basin-threshold sweeps, LISTEN ablations, ending thresholds.
- **Design constraint (Ben, 14 Nov 2026):** do not make the player's freedom depend on choosing the
  compassionate action; cooperation's advantages are to be discovered through consequences, with
  genuinely useful offensive options retained.
